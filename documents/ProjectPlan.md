# Project Plan

## Vision

Develop a research-quality electrothermal simulator capable of modeling hotspot formation, vortex dynamics, Josephson effects, and eventually TDGL dynamics in superconducting thin films.

## Primary Objectives

- Accurate electrothermal model
- TDGL implementation
- Josephson junction framework
- Validation against experiment

## Long-Term Goals

- Publishable research
- GPU acceleration
- Experimental comparison
- Modular physics engine
